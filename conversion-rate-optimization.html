Conversion Rate Optimization FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about our CRO services, testing and optimization approach.

Conversion rate optimization is the process of improving website pages and user journeys so a higher percentage of visitors complete actions such as enquiries, registrations or purchases.

A CRO audit reviews analytics, conversion funnels, landing pages, forms, calls to action, mobile usability, navigation, page speed and customer friction to identify improvement opportunities.

A/B testing compares two versions of a page element with real users. It helps identify whether a change to messaging, design, form or call to action produces better conversion results.

Initial improvements can be implemented after the audit, but reliable test results depend on website traffic and conversion volume. CRO is most effective as an ongoing research and testing process.
