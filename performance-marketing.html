Performance Marketing FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about our performance marketing services and approach.

Performance marketing is a data-driven approach in which campaigns are planned, measured and optimised around specific results such as leads, purchases, app actions or revenue.

We manage relevant paid channels such as Google Search, Display, Shopping, YouTube, Meta and remarketing, selecting the mix according to your audience, offer and campaign goals.

We configure platform conversion actions and analytics using tools such as GA4 and Google Tag Manager to measure forms, calls, purchases and other valuable customer actions.

Campaigns can begin generating traffic after approval, but reliable optimisation requires sufficient click and conversion data. Performance generally improves through ongoing testing and refinement.

We track metrics relevant to your goals, including conversions, cost per lead, cost per acquisition, conversion rate, revenue and return on ad spend, along with lead quality.

Advertising spend is normally paid directly to the selected platforms and is separate from campaign management charges. We recommend a budget based on goals, market and competition.
