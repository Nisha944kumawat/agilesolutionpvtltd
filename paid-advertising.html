About Our Paid Advertising Services

Agile Edtech Solutions provides end-to-end paid advertising services — campaign strategy, audience research, creative, media buying and conversion rate optimisation. We run high-performing Search, Shopping, Social (Meta, LinkedIn, TikTok), Programmatic and Video campaigns with rigorous tracking, A/B testing and weekly/monthly reporting to maximize ROI.

  • Audience & Remarketing — retention and ROAS improvements
  • Budget & Bid Management — smart bidding and spend efficiency
